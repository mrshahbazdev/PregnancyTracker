# Privacy Policy

**Pregnancy Tracker** is built with privacy as a core principle.

## Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on external servers. All data entered into the app — including your pregnancy profile, symptom logs, kick counts, measurements, journal entries, photos, and AI assistant API key — is stored locally on your device only.

## AI Assistant

The AI assistant uses a "Bring Your Own Key" (BYOK) model. Your OpenAI or Google Gemini API key is stored only in your device's secure enclave (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android) and is never sent to our servers or any third party other than the AI provider you choose.

## Photos and Backups

Bump photos are stored on your device. Backup and restore features save a JSON file on your device or a location you choose; we do not access it.

## Notifications

Local notifications are scheduled and delivered on your device. We do not use them to collect data.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as the app evolves. The latest version will always be available within the app and at the app's store listing.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer through the Google Play Store listing.

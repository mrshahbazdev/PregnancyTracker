# Privacy Policy — Pregnancy Tracker

**Effective date:** July 5, 2026

Pregnancy Tracker ("the app") is an offline-first pregnancy companion app. Your privacy is our highest priority.

## Data Collection

The app does **not** collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information you enter — including your name, due date, kick counts, contractions, symptoms, mood, weight, blood pressure, notes, and photos — is stored **only on your device**.

- We have no servers. Your data never leaves your phone.
- We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
- We do not require an account or sign-in.

## AI Assistant (Bring Your Own Key)

The optional AI Assistant feature lets you provide your own OpenAI or Google Gemini API key. If you choose to use it:

- Your API key is stored only on your device in the secure Android Keystore.
- Your questions are sent directly from your device to the AI provider you selected (OpenAI or Google), governed by that provider's privacy policy.
- We never see, store, or transmit your key or your conversations.

## Backups and Sharing

Backup export and sharing features are entirely user-initiated. Data is only exported or shared when you explicitly choose to do so, to the destination you select.

## Children's Privacy

The app is intended for adults (expectant parents) and is not directed at children under 13.

## Medical Disclaimer

The app provides educational information only and is not medical advice or a diagnostic tool. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider.
